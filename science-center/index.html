Science Center

Scientific papers, explained in short videos

Science Center turns scientific papers into short narrated explainer videos, in two versions: Simply Explained for a general audience and Deep Dive, step by step, for technical viewers. Each is made in English, French and Arabic and published on the Science Center YouTube channels.

Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. The narration is a synthetic voice and the animations are generated from the paper; each video's description says so, and credits and links the original paper.

Science Center publisher is the private tool that uploads approved videos to our own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.
